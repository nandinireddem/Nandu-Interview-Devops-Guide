Kubernetes DevOps Interview Guide

Complete Kubernetes preparation covering fundamentals, workloads, services, storage, security, scheduling, autoscaling, ingress, Helm, monitoring, troubleshooting and real-time scenarios.

🎯 Kubernetes Learning Path

Study the main concepts first, then move to production concepts and finally revise the real-time scenarios before interviews.

☸️

01. Kubernetes Basics

Kubernetes architecture, cluster components, Pods, Nodes, Namespaces, kubectl and core concepts.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ MUST KNOW
🚀

02. Workloads

Pods, Deployments, ReplicaSets, StatefulSets, DaemonSets, Jobs, CronJobs and workload management.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ MUST KNOW
🌐

03. Services & Networking

ClusterIP, NodePort, LoadBalancer, service discovery, DNS, networking and traffic flow.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ MUST KNOW
🔑

04. ConfigMaps & Secrets

Application configuration, environment variables, Secrets, volume mounts and secure configuration management.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ IMPORTANT
💾

05. Storage: PV & PVC

PersistentVolumes, PersistentVolumeClaims, StorageClasses, dynamic provisioning and persistent application storage.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ MUST KNOW
📦

06. Configuration & Storage

Additional configuration and storage concepts used in real Kubernetes deployments.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ SUPPORTING
🔐

07. RBAC & Security

Users, Roles, RoleBindings, ServiceAccounts, permissions, security practices and access control.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ IMPORTANT
⚡

08. Probes, Scheduling & Autoscaling

Liveness, readiness, startup probes, scheduling, taints, tolerations, affinity and HPA.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ IMPORTANT
📊

09. Ingress, Helm & Monitoring

Ingress, Helm charts, releases, Prometheus, Grafana and Kubernetes observability concepts.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ IMPORTANT
🔥

10. Troubleshooting & Real-Time

CrashLoopBackOff, ImagePullBackOff, Pending Pods, networking, resource issues and production troubleshooting scenarios.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ INTERVIEW
🎯

11. Final Revision

Overall Kubernetes revision covering architecture, workloads, networking, storage, security, troubleshooting and key commands.

🔥 FINAL REVISION
🔥 Recommended Interview Order

Basics → Workloads → Services & Networking → ConfigMaps & Secrets → Storage → RBAC & Security → Probes & Scheduling → Ingress & Helm → Troubleshooting → Final Revision

Tip: Don't memorize every Kubernetes command. Understand the architecture, traffic flow, workload behavior and troubleshooting approach.
